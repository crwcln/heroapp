About Hero

Hero takes its name from Herodotus, the ancient Greek writer history remembers as its first great chronicler. He traveled, asked questions, and wrote down what he found, which is the whole idea here too.

This is a map quiz built to make geography and history stick: name a place, find a place, revisit the ones you keep missing. Clio, the muse of history, is around if you want a memory trick or a quick answer.
